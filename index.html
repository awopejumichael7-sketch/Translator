          Help

          How to translate audio

          1. Choose your audio. Upload a file, record with your microphone, or drop a file onto the Translate page.
          2. Pick the languages. Choose what is spoken, and what you want it translated into.
          3. Press Translate audio. The app transcribes, translates and prepares the voice. The first time, it asks before downloading AI models.
          4. Listen and save. Play the translation, fix any words in the text boxes, and download what you need.

          Common questions

          Why does the app ask to download models?

          The listening, translating and speaking are done by AI models that run on your device. They are large, so the app tells you the size first. After the download they work without internet.

          Can I use a YouTube link?

          The app does not take audio from YouTube. Download or save the video in a way YouTube permits, then upload the file here.

          Does it work with WhatsApp voice notes?

          Yes. Save the voice note to your phone, then choose it with Upload file. If your browser cannot read it, convert it to MP3 or M4A first.

          Why can I not download audio for some languages?

          Browser voices can be played but not saved as a file. A downloadable voice needs a voice model. Where no voice model exists for a language, the app shows the translated text and tells you the voice is unavailable.

          Why is Igbo speech not recognised?

          The speech models used here do not include Igbo. You can still type or paste Igbo text and translate it, and Igbo can be a target language.

          The translation is not perfect.

          Automatic translation can make mistakes, especially for names and scripture. Read the result, and use Edit to correct it before you share it.

          Does a video get a translated soundtrack?

          No. The app produces a separate translated audio file. It does not replace the audio inside your video.

          Can I use it offline?

          The app itself opens offline after the first visit. Translating offline needs the models you use to be downloaded already.

          Install on your device

          Use the Install app button when it appears. On iPhone or iPad, open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. On desktop Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar.

          Privacy

          • Your audio is processed locally whenever supported. We do not intentionally upload your recordings to a third-party server.
          • AI model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face and the code that runs them from jsDelivr. These downloads never include your audio or text.
          • The online translation service is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, only the text is sent, to MyMemory.
          • History, settings and models are stored in this browser. Use Clear all data in Settings to remove them.

          Open-source components

          • Whisper speech recognition (MIT) by OpenAI, run with Transformers.js (Apache-2.0).
          • NLLB-200 translation by Meta (CC-BY-NC-4.0, non-commercial use).
          • Opus-MT translation models by the Helsinki-NLP group (CC-BY-4.0).
          • MMS-TTS voices by Meta (CC-BY-NC-4.0, non-commercial use).

          Some models are licensed for non-commercial use. Use them for church and community work, not for selling a service.
